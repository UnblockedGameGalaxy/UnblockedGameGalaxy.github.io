Game Overview

Alien Hominid is the legendary Newgrounds run-and-gun Flash shooter created by Tom Fulp and Dan Paladin (The Behemoth). After your UFO crash-lands on Earth, fight back against relentless waves of FBI agents, burrow underground to ambush enemies, leap onto agents' heads, and hijack vehicles to reclaim your spaceship!

About Alien Hominid

Developed by: Tom Fulp & Dan Paladin (The Behemoth)

Packed with hand-drawn animation, frantic arcade shooting, power-up blasters, and over-the-top boss fights, the original Flash prototype of Alien Hominid became a cornerstone of browser gaming before expanding into a full console hit.

How to Play

  • Move & Aim: Use the Arrow Keys to run, aim upward/diagonally, or duck
  • Shoot & Melee: Press A to fire your blaster (hold to charge a super shot, or press close-up to slash enemies)
  • Jump, Roll & Grenade: Press S to jump, D to throw grenades, and Down + S to burrow underground
