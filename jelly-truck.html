Game Overview

Jelly Truck is the quirky soft-body physics driving Flash game created by GameTornado. Steer a squishy, rubbery truck through a world made entirely of wobbly jelly—climbing giant rotating gears, bouncing across springy platforms, and squeezing past crushing blocks to reach the checkered flag at the end of all 20 levels!

About Jelly Truck

Developed by: GameTornado

Because both your truck and the terrain deform and bounce on impact, smooth throttle control and careful weight shifting are the keys to climbing steep jelly ramps without flipping upside down.

How to Play

  • Drive & Reverse: Press the Up Arrow (or W) to accelerate forward and the Down Arrow (or S) to brake or reverse
  • Balance & Tilt: Use the Left and Right Arrow Keys (or A / D) to lean your jelly truck and keep your wheels gripping wobbly obstacles
  • Reach the Finish Flag: Navigate past moving pistons, spinning cogs, and teetering bridges to cross the goal line on every stage
