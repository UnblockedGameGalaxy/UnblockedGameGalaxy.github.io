About Slope

Slope is the iconic infinite ball-roller where players steer a fast-moving ball through a neon futuristic course. Use the arrow keys or A/D to dodge obstacles and stay on the track as long as possible.

How to Play

  • A / Left Arrow: Steer Left
  • D / Right Arrow: Steer Right
  • Avoid red blocks at all costs!
