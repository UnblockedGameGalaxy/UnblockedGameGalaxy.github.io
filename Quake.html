Game Overview

Quake is id Software's groundbreaking 1996 3D first-person shooter running directly in your browser via NetQuake. Step through slipgates into dark, Lovecraftian realms, collect iconic weapons like the Super Nailgun, Thunderbolt, and Rocket Launcher, and blast your way through hordes of shamblers, fiends, and death knights in single-player or multiplayer arenas.

How to Play

  • Move & Aim: Click inside the game window to lock your mouse cursor for 360-degree aiming, and use W, A, S, D (or the Arrow Keys) to move and strafe
  • Shoot & Jump: Press Left Click (or Ctrl) to fire your weapon and Spacebar to jump across gaps and ledges
  • Switch Weapons & Menu: Press number keys 1–8 to swap weapons and Esc to open the in-game menu or adjust mouse sensitivity
