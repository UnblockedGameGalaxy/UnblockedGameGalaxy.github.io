Game Overview

ScratchNite brings top-down Fortnite-style Battle Royale action to Scratch, running smoothly at 60 FPS on TurboWarp. How to Play

  • Move & Aim: Use W, A, S, D (or the Arrow Keys) to move around the map and aim with your Mouse cursor
  • Shoot & Harvest: Left-Click to fire your equipped weapon or swing your pickaxe to gather building materials and open chests
  • Switch Slots & Build: Use the Number Keys (1-5) or scroll wheel to cycle weapons and healing items, and stay inside the safe zone as the storm closes in
