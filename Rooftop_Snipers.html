Game Overview

Rooftop Snipers is a chaotic, two-button pixel-style physics duel where your sole objective is to blast your opponent off the roof. Time your jumps to dodge incoming bullets, hold your fire button to angle your rifle, and be the first to score 5 knockoffs!

How to Play

  • Player 1 Controls: Press W to jump and dodge, and hold E to raise your weapon and release to shoot
