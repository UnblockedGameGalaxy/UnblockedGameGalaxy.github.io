Game Overview

Bloons Tower Defense 3 is Ninja Kiwi's massively upgraded third entry in the iconic Flash tower defense series. Tackle 8 distinct tracks across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties using dual upgrade paths, brand-new towers like the Spike-o-pult and Monkey Beacon, and explosive Pineapple Bombs to survive Ceramic bloons and the massive MOAB blimp!

About Bloons Tower Defense 3

Developed by: Ninja Kiwi

Featuring two separate upgrade paths per tower—including Super Monkey Plasma Vision, Triple Darts, and Glaive Ricochet—Bloons TD 3 lets you build devastating monkey defenses and customize targeting priorities across 50 intense rounds.

How to Play

  • Pick a Track & Mode: Choose any of the 8 tracks on Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty
  • Build & Dual-Upgrade Towers: Left Click a tower from the sidebar to place it on the map, then click placed towers to invest in two distinct upgrade trees
  • Deploy Track Hazards: Place Road Spikes, Monkey Glue, and timed Pineapple Bombs on the path to pop tough Lead, Ceramic, and MOAB bloons
